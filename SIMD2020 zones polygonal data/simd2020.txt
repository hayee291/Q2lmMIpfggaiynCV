b)	SIMD:
Download: https://www.macs.hw.ac.uk/~pb56/bigdatamanagementcw2/simd2020.sql 
•	A copy of the Scottish Index of Multiple Deprivation 2020 to be loaded into PostgreSQL with a geometry column (i.e. requires the PostGIS extension)
•	Social Index of Multiple Deprivation (SIMD) is a polygonal spatial dataset which shows the affluence of regions across the city of Edinburgh. 
•	It uses the same coordinate system (EPSG:4326) as the lat/lng coordinates of the bus dataset
•	Source: https://www.gov.scot/collections/scottish-index-of-multiple-deprivation-2020
